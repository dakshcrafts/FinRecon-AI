Gripher is an enterprise-grade financial reconciliation platform engineered for PS02 — Invoice & Payment Reconciliation. It operates 100% locally with zero cloud leakage, resolving multi-invoice splits, partial settlements, duplicate candidates, and mathematical discrepancies with explainable auditability.

Multi-Pass Matching Architecture
Pass 1: Direct 1-to-1 Deterministic Matching

Extracts exact invoice reference tokens from transaction narratives, computes RapidFuzz normalized Levenshtein ratios on customer names, verifies date proximity within configurable tolerance windows, and checks exact unallocated amounts with integer-cent minor currency precision.

Ambiguity Margin Protection

If two competing payments score within the ambiguity margin (≤ 5.0%), the engine demotes the match to Medium Confidence and routes it to the human-in-the-loop Review Queue, preventing accidental misallocation.

Pass 2: Partial & Split Settlement Intelligence

Receipts smaller than open invoice balances are allocated partially without balance loss. Single lump-sum payments spanning multiple invoices for the same customer are systematically decomposed across open accounts.

Pass 3: Rule-Based Anomaly & Risk Detection

Flags identical deposits within 14 days as duplicate candidates, isolates credit vs debit withdrawals, prevents allocations beyond remaining balances, and detects severe invoice aging risks.

Data Privacy & Offline Security

Gripher is designed with local-first financial sovereignty. All ledger balances, audit trails, and parsing pipelines run locally without sending unencrypted data to external third parties.

  • Database Engine: SQLite 3 in WAL mode with Foreign Key integrity constraints.
  • Security Layer: Bcrypt password hashing, local JWT authorization, and parameterized SQL operations.
  • Network Binding: Bound strictly to 127.0.0.1 to prevent network exposure.
